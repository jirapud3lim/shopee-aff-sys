# Shopee Affiliate Studio agent instructions

Follow the project's [operating instructions](CLAUDE.md). Apply the product rules to every Studio product, regardless of category or discovery source. Apply the Google Flow workflow whenever preparing or generating video in Flow, including a single insert, a batch of scenes, or an added shot.

## Production deployment

Keep Codex Computer Use out of production artifacts and runtime, including web, API, workers, scheduled jobs and delegated services. Production workflows use supported APIs or operator handoffs. See the [pipeline audit](docs/product/COMPUTER_USE_AUDIT.md) and [open production tickets](docs/backlog/PRODUCTION_NO_COMPUTER_USE.md).

## Product pipeline rules

1. **Keep the same product throughout.** Verify the Shopee Shop ID, Item ID, and selected variant where applicable. Carry that identity through Affiliate link capture, storyboard, export, and publication. Verify that the saved link reaches the selected listing and is associated with the intended channel. If the listing opens a shared variant picker, state the selected variant clearly in the video and caption where applicable.
2. **Review the exported video.** Watch and listen to the complete MP4 before handing it off. Check the product against its references, spoken brand and model names, variants and numbers, audio gaps, subtitle timing, and text placement against the destination's mobile player controls where applicable.
3. **Retry only the affected work.** Preserve approved shots and audio. Fix an incorrect or missing line by replacing only that audio. Check the existing generation status and available credits before resubmitting, then retry only the affected shot or line.
4. **Confirm publication separately.** Require explicit authorization for live publication. Verify that the video version, destination account or Page, caption, required disclosures, and destination link match the authorized scope. Apply the [AI content and disclosure rules](#ai-content-and-disclosure). After publishing, verify the live post and any requested Affiliate comment separately.
5. **Keep sources and approvals traceable.** Associate original product references, storyboard panels, source clips, and narration with the same product and shot numbers. Record the approved versions and keep the exported review file identifiable so an individual shot can be reused or revised.

## AI content and disclosure

- **Check both disclosures before publishing.** Review the final video for realistic AI-generated or materially AI-altered visuals and audio, including Flow footage and synthetic narration even when based on real product photos. Check the destination's current AI-label and commercial-disclosure requirements separately. Enable each applicable platform control. In the caption, identify which elements used AI and disclose potential Affiliate commission where applicable. If the chosen publishing path cannot apply a required label or disclosure, keep the post as a draft for completion through a supported path.
- **TikTok.** Enable **AI-generated content** for realistic AI imagery, video, or audio. Enable **Disclose commercial content** for product promotions and select **Branded content** when promoting a third party for Affiliate commission. [TikTok AI labeling](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content) · [TikTok commercial disclosure](https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-and-business-accounts/promoting-a-brand-product-or-service)
- **Facebook and Instagram Reels.** For organic posts, use Meta's AI disclosure tool for digitally generated or altered photorealistic video or realistic-sounding audio. Separately apply any commercial disclosure the destination requires. [Meta AI disclosure](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/) · [Meta AI info label](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/04/metas-approach-to-labeling-ai-generated-content-and-manipulated-media/)
- **Shopee Video campaigns.** Check the terms of the specific campaign before submission. Exclude AI content from Mission Shopee Video when its terms require self-created, non-AI videos. [Mission Shopee Video terms](https://help.shopee.co.th/portal/10/article/148841-Mission-Shopee-Video)
- **Keep claims verifiable.** Use images, clips, music, voices, and likenesses you have rights to use. Describe product details supported by the exact listing or actual footage. Claim personal use or test results only when the experience or test actually occurred. Do not use synthetic media to impersonate a real person or fabricate an endorsement or product result. [TikTok AI content restrictions](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/ai-generated-content)

## Product videos and voiceovers

- For every product video or voiceover, including audio-only edits and retries, use the saved Google Flow character [พิธีกรคัดของเข้าบ้าน](https://flow.google.com/project/81d65799-15e1-4834-b708-4e17857d09ff/character/0e74b82c-79cb-43da-badb-dc5e6d520d35) and its assigned **Callirrhoe** voice. Use the saved character wherever a presenter appears. For product-only shots with narration, use Callirrhoe off-screen.
- Reuse approved character references and existing Callirrhoe recordings when they match the shot or script. For new dialogue, synthesize only the lines needed with the saved voice. For narration revisions, keep the approved footage and replace only the affected audio. Verify the character or voice selection before submitting generation or synthesis.
- If the saved character or its voice cannot be selected or verified, pause the affected generation and tell the user. Creating a replacement character or using another voice requires explicit user approval.

## Google Flow workflow

1. **Analyze the product and prepare reference images.** Use the first prompt in [Master Prompt](Shopee_Studio_Reel_Master_Prompt_v2.md#2-สองพรอมต์ก่อนสร้างวิดีโอ) with original photos of the exact listing variant. Record visible details and uncertainties, then create standalone photorealistic product reference stills. Review each generated image against the originals and show the reference set before moving to the storyboard. Retain the originals for identity checks. When the user asks to see the references first, stop at this stage.
2. **Storyboard from the reviewed reference set.** Send a separate prompt attaching the references that passed the product checks and the saved presenter's reference when the shot calls for one. Ask Flow Agent for still images only, with one numbered panel framed vertically at 9:16 for each planned shot. Record the planned duration, action, and dialogue or voiceover for each shot that needs it. Choose the number and length of shots for the brief.
   - **Photorealistic stills only.** Require every panel to look like a real photograph, including product close-ups, inserts, and revisions. In the Flow prompt, specify natural lighting and shadows, realistic textures and proportions, and physically plausible poses and contact where applicable. Exclude illustrations, cartoons, sketches, and visibly stylized renders.
3. **Review and stop.** Show the actual storyboard images and shot order to the user. Check the product model, color, packaging and label, item count, presenter consistency, and any hold or hand action against the references. Flag visible mismatches and regenerate the affected storyboard panels as needed. Wait for the user to explicitly approve the presented version or the specific shots before sending a video-generation command.
   - Check each panel for photorealism before presenting the storyboard for approval. Revise any panel that fails this check.
4. **Generate only the approved scope in 9:16 with Veo 3.1 Lite.** After approval, select and verify both settings in Flow before every video-generation request, including retries. Send a separate Flow Agent command listing the approved shot numbers and asking it to generate those videos in order. If either required setting is unavailable or cannot be verified, pause and tell the user. If a shot changes or a new shot is requested, create and show its storyboard panel, then wait for approval of that panel before generating it.
5. **Verify and download.** Check that Flow returned the approved shots in vertical 9:16 and that the output still matches the references. Keep the storyboard shot numbers in the clip names or download order so the clips can be assembled correctly.
